Charge Smart Site Plan

Site Name

Charge Smart

This name tells visitors the site is about charging an iPhone the smart way. It is short, easy to remember, and points to the main goal: helping people look after their battery.

Site Purpose

Charge Smart explains iPhone battery health in plain language and helps people take better care of their battery. The site has a Home page that explains what battery health means, a Battery Tips page with simple charging habits and settings, and a Battery Check page where visitors enter their battery health percentage and phone age to get general advice. Their recent results are saved on their device. The advice is general guidance, not a diagnosis.

Scenarios

  • What does the battery health percentage on my iPhone actually mean?
  • Is my battery worn enough that I should look into a service or replacement?
  • Which charging habits help my battery last longer?
  • What results did I get the last time I checked my battery?
